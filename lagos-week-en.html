Sat 10/10east
Benagil skylight + Seven Hanging Valleys + pottery town + Ramires Praia da Marinha 45-min drive to the Marinha car park · Porches and Guia on the way back

Morning: park at Praia da Marinha and walk a stretch of the Seven Hanging Valleys Trail toward Benagil (40 min one way — Portugal's finest coastal path), ending at the skylight above the Benagil cave, looking down through the dome onto the beach inside. To go inside, catch a 30–40 minute small boat from Benagil beach (cheap; mornings are quieter). Two stops on the way back: ① Porches Pottery on the EN125 — a 1968 workshop where painters work in the shop; they ship large pieces to Canada. ② Ramires in Guia (open 1964, the self-proclaimed birthplace of piri piri chicken) — September was Bonjardim in Lisbon; today is the pilgrimage version. Home by late afternoon.

Sun 10/11west
Three fishing villages + Sagres + Cape St. Vincent sunset (one westward line) Praia da Luz Luz 15 min → Burgau → Salema → Sagres (Fortaleza de Sagres) → Cabo de Sao Vicente · west all day, no backtracking

Out by 10:00. String the villages first: a stroll at Praia da Luz, photos in Burgau (whitewashed houses squeezed into a cove), then seafood lunch in Salema — fishing boats parked right on the sand. Push 20 minutes further west to Sagres: the fortress alone on its headland (Henry the Navigator's legendary school), then an afternoon on Praia do Tonel or Mareta watching the surfers — your friend's "sunset on the beach" coast. Toward evening, Cape St. Vincent: 140-metre cliffs, a lighthouse, the southwest corner of Europe. Sunset around 18:55, straight into the Atlantic; 40 minutes home in the dark. Windy and cold — jackets mandatory.

PTO

The rental (book today): pick up and drop off at a Lagos town office — Saturday morning to Monday morning (offices are mostly closed Sunday evening, so 48 hours lands perfectly; Monday's drop-off is a 10-minute errand to open the flop day). Automatic. Take the N125 to both Benagil and Sagres and the A22 toll-tag question never comes up.

Saturday's boat: to go inside the Benagil cave, the safest play is booking a morning small-boat slot (the 30–40 min kind from Benagil beach) on GetYourGuide/Viator the night before; walk-up works too if you're early. The free view down through the skylight is already spectacular.

Sunday means jackets: Sagres and the cape are windy and cold, and the temperature drops fast after sunset. Those beaches are for looking — swimming stays in Lagos.

Pottery logistics: have Porches ship anything big; small pieces fly carry-on — Oct 16 is Air Transat, far more generous than Ryanair was.
