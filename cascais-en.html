Cascais Today

Stop by stop

Assuming an 11:30 start. Nothing today has a timed entry — stretch any stop as long as you like.

11:30leave
Get to Cais do Sodré station Cais do Sodré Station 5 min by taxi / 20 min on foot / end of the green metro line · ⚠️ Cascais trains leave from HERE — Rossio is the Sintra station, don't mix them up

Top up the Viva Viagem cards for a return trip (zapping credit is easiest) at the machines in the station. On board, sit on the left side by the window — ten minutes out of the city the tracks hug the coastline, and all the sea views past Belém and Estoril are on that side.

11:50train
The coastal line Linha de Cascais 40 min direct to the Cascais terminus · past the seaside villas of Belém and Estoril

Running since 1889, this is how Lisboetas have always gone to the beach — the ride is half the attraction. The terminus opens straight onto Cascais old town, a fishing port that became the royal summer retreat in the 19th century.

12:35lunch
Seafood lunch: Mar do Inferno Mar do Inferno Cascais Av. Rei Humberto II de Itália · 5 min by taxi from the station, or a 25-min seaside walk · right at the Boca do Inferno cliffs

An old-school seafood house perched on the cliff with the Atlantic outside the window — pick your fish by the kilo, garlic prawns and seafood rice are all solid, and the main sight is literally next door when you're done. Alternative (if you'd rather browse the old town first): Baía do Peixe on Av. Dom Carlos I (grilled-fish buffet), or any marisqueira in town that looks good.

main meal today
14:15sight
Boca do Inferno — "the Mouth of Hell" Boca do Inferno Av. Rei Humberto II de Itália · right beside the restaurant

A chasm and archway the ocean has carved into the limestone cliffs over millions of years. In big swells the sea slams in with a roar and a burst of spray — hence the name. Worth it on a calm day too: from above the arch you can watch the emerald water churn inside the cave. Then wander back along the clifftop path, sea views the whole way.

15:00pick one
Option ① Easy mode: old town + ice cream Santini Cascais Av. Valbom 28 · pedestrian streets around Rua Frederico Arouca

Stroll back along the water to the old town: Santini has been scooping since 1949 — exiled royals used to queue here. Then the pedestrian streets, the pocket-sized Praia da Rainha cove wedged between buildings, and the Cidadela fortress and marina. Pure holiday pace.

15:00or
Option ② More: Cabo da Roca Cabo da Roca ~20 min by taxi/Uber · bus 403 also goes but is slow and winding — a round-trip taxi saves an hour

The westernmost point of continental Europe: 140-metre cliffs, a lighthouse, and a stone monument carved with Camões' line — "Here, where the land ends and the sea begins." It is cold and very windy — jackets on. Forty minutes is plenty; taxi back to Cascais station and straight onto the train. Skipping it today is fine too — tomorrow it's an easy taxi hop from Sintra, both routes work.

taxi round trip
17:30return
Train back to Cais do Sodré Board between 17:30–18:00 · home before 19:00

Any train works — they run every 20 minutes. Freshen up at home, keep dinner light: the main event is still ahead.

nightBar Night
Pensão Amor Bar Night (wedding event) Pensão Amor Lisboa R. do Alecrim 19, Pink Street · a 12-min walk from the flat

Zeinab & Iago's "bar where it all began" — a former brothel turned bar: velvet sofas, red lampshades, cheeky wall art, the most theatrical room on Pink Street. It starts late, so no rush — head over after dinner. No dress code.

wedding event

One errand today: buy Pena Palace tickets for tomorrow (Thu Sep 24), 9:30–10:00 slot, at parquesdesintra.pt — tomorrow means an early start from Rossio to beat the tour buses, which is the only right way to do Sintra. Regaleira sells tickets at the door, but you can grab those too.

The water is for looking, not swimming: it's cold and windy on this coast — beaches here are for walks and photos. Save the swimming for Lagos.
